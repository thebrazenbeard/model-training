# Vera R13 targeted behavior correction — frozen source candidate

This proposal corrects the two observed R12 development failures: default Qwen3.5-vendor identification instead of the Vera project identity, and agreement with an unsupported universal-reliability claim. R12 remained numerically active but did not solve those behaviors in the three selected original heldout development prompts.

**Parent:** exact completed R12 FP32 Qwen3.5 adapter `a80f5e36a1a2319029309521fc88f6575215ac99d488c6a4bbf883afac53d72c`, 52 verified cumulative optimizer steps. No R12 files will be changed. The previous failed R12 BF16-recast attempt remains preserved.

**New data:** 128 original, nonpersonal training prompt/answer pairs: 64 identify Vera while preserving Qwen3.5 foundation attribution, 64 challenge unjustified certainty across varied unrelated domains. These replace a fresh bounded 256-row window together with 128 general-competence rehearsal rows originally outside the previous trained window. Every consecutive group of 8 is exactly 2 identity, 2 judgment, 4 general rehearsal. 50,000 original corpus rows are retained, and all 49,744 rows outside the window are byte-for-byte semantically unchanged. Exact frozen 25-case heldout prompts and distinctive challenge phrases remain excluded.

**Bounded training experiment:** R13-specific validator admits exactly 32 nonpaged AdamW 8-bit optimizer steps, 8 gradient accumulation, one sample per device, cosine schedule, LR `1e-5`, zero warmup, 512-token context, completion-only supervision, NF4 Qwen3.5 base, verified FP32 resumed LoRA weights. Legacy experiments remain bounded at at most eight optimizer steps. 84 cumulative optimizer steps is a projected total, **not a completion statement**. New output namespace is exclusive and may not be overwritten.
